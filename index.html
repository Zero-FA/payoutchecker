        Simple Explanations of Compliance Rules
        👉 Click to expand
        ▶
        These are the trading-behavior rules Apex checks during payout review. This is the simplified version.
        Contract Scaling Rule
        Trade only within the contract limits for your account size. Exceeding the limit results in denial.
        Example: On a 50k PA you're allowed 5 minis until passing trailing DD + $100. Using 8 minis before qualifying → scaling violation → payout denied.
        ▼ View contract scaling limits
        BEFORE reaching Trailing DD + $100:
        25k — 2 minis
        50k — 5 minis
        100k — 7 minis
        150k — 8 minis
        250k — 13 minis
        300k — 17 minis
        100k Static — 2 minis (no scaling)

        AFTER reaching Trailing DD + $100:
        25k — 4 minis
        50k — 10 minis
        100k — 14 minis
        150k — 17 minis
        250k — 27 minis
        300k — 35 minis
        100k Static — 2 minis
        Full contract size only allowed after passing trailing DD + $100.
        30% Consistency Rule (Windfall Rule) — Automatically Checked
        Your largest winning day cannot exceed 30% of your total accumulated profit. This prevents payouts based on one oversized day instead of consistent performance.
        Example:
        Total profit: $1,000 → max allowed winning day = $300
        Largest winning day = $450 → Fails
        Largest winning day = $250 → Passes
        30% Negative P&L Rule (Maximum Adverse Excursion)
        No trade may exceed 30% of start-of-day profit in open negative P&L.
        Example: Start-of-day profit: $4,000 → MAE limit = $1,200.
        Low/new profit accounts use 30% of the trailing threshold.
        If EOD profit > 2× safety net → MAE becomes 50% next session.
        Static: Below $2,600 → MAE = $187.50. Above $2,600 → MAE = 30%.
        ▼ View MAE thresholds by account size
        MAE is normally 30% of start-of-day profit (or the “low/new profit” trailing-threshold-based limit).

        When it increases:
        If EOD profit > 2× safety net → next session MAE can become 50% (per rule conditions).

        Quick reference by PA size (safety net context):
        $25k — Safety net = $1,600 (2× = $3,200)
        $50k — Safety net = $2,600 (2× = $5,200)
        $100k — Safety net = $3,100 (2× = $6,200)
        $150k — Safety net = $5,100 (2× = $10,200)
        $250k — Safety net = $6,600 (2× = $13,200)
        $300k — Safety net = $7,600 (2× = $15,200)
        $100k Static — Safety net = $2,600 (2× = $5,200)

        Static special case:
        Below $2,600 → MAE = $187.50
        Above $2,600 → MAE = 30%
        5:1 Risk–Reward Ratio Rule
        Your stop loss cannot be significantly larger than your profit target. Apex requires that your stop be no more than 5 times your target.
        Examples:
        Target $100, Stop $700 → 7× target → Fails
        Target $100, Stop $500 → 5× target → Passes
        Target $200, Stop $900 → 4.5× target → Passes
        Hedging Rule
        You cannot be long and short at the same time on the same or correlated markets.
        Example: Long ES + Short MES → hedging → violation.
        ▼ View correlated instrument groups
        U.S. Indices: ES, NQ, YM, RTY, EMD, MES, MNQ, MYM, M2K
        Metals: GC, SI, HG, etc.
        Energy: CL, QM, RB, NG, etc.
        Currencies: 6A, 6B, 6E, etc.
        Crypto: MBT, MET
        Euro Bonds: FGBL, FGBM, FGBX
        If two instruments are in the same group, do NOT long one and short the other.
        One Direction Rule
        Apex requires you to trade in one direction at a time. You can be long or short, but not both at once. You also cannot use a strategy that places active entry orders on both sides of the market at the same time.
        Exit orders (profit targets and stop-losses) are allowed. The restriction only applies to strategies that attempt to work both sides of the market simultaneously.
        Examples:
        • Long ES → using sell orders to close the long = Allowed
        • Short NQ → using buy orders to close the short = Allowed
        • Long ES + Short MES at the same time = Not allowed
        • Having active buy AND sell entry orders on the same market = Not allowed
